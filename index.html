maNOWj GeoIntel

A map that answers questions about a place. Pick a point anywhere in the world and ask what is there, how many hospitals are within five kilometres, what the weather is doing, or how to get there. Every answer shows the source it came from and the date it applies to.

What you can do

  • Count hospitals, schools, shops, ATMs and other places within a radius of any point.
  • Get directions and live turn-by-turn navigation.
  • See weather, air quality and rain radar for a location.
  • Look up population, area and density, always with the census year attached.
  • Switch to satellite view or 3D buildings, and see when the satellite image was taken.
  • Measure a distance, or draw a shape to measure an area.
  • Play maNOWj Daily: five satellite views a day, guess where each one is.

Where the data comes from

OpenStreetMap and Nominatim for places and addresses, Overpass for map features, OSRM for routing, Open-Meteo for weather and air quality, USGS for earthquakes, Wikidata for population, and Esri and NASA for satellite imagery. Nothing is estimated: where a source has no figure, the app says so instead of guessing.
